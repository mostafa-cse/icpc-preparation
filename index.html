Ground Rules

  • Time-box in training, not in contests. Cap yourself at 45–60 min on a problem before taking a hint during grind blocks. During live contests, sit through the whole thing — no early peeking, that's the muscle you're building.
  • Upsolve same day, every time. A problem you finish two days later barely counts — the value is in closing the loop while the contest is still fresh.
  • Keep an error log. The 2–3 line note at the end of each grind day matters more than it feels like it does. Recurring mistakes are worth more than new problems solved.
  • One rest half-day every two weeks, non-negotiable. This is a marathon, not a sprint — burnout in month three costs more than the half-day ever will.
  • Have teammates? This plan is written for solo pacing. Sync the mock-contest blocks (the DP block onward, and the final block) with your team explicitly — one shared machine, rotate who's typing, practice the handoff, not just the algorithms.
